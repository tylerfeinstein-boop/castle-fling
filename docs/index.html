Privacy Policy · Terms & Conditions

The short version

Castle Fling has no developer-operated backend server. We do not run accounts, and we do not collect, transmit, or store your personal information on any server we control. Your game progress — coins, crowns, meta-progression unlocks, and ad-free status — is stored locally on your device only. If you uninstall the game, that local data is removed (except where your device's own Android backup service restores it).

Data we collect directly

None. We do not collect your name, email address, account information, contacts, location, or any other personal data. The game does not require you to sign in.

Third-party services

Castle Fling uses the following third-party services, which may collect certain data as described by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Billing — processes optional in-app purchases (crown packs, the game's premium currency). Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never receive or store your card or payment details. Google provides us only a record that a purchase occurred so the game can grant it.
    Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — serves interstitial and optional rewarded ads. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, device identifiers, IP address, and app usage data (such as ad interactions) to serve and, where permitted, personalize ads.
    How Google uses data in ads

Controlling ad personalization

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization at any time in your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads (on most Android devices), where you can choose "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization." Purchasing any crown pack in Castle Fling also permanently removes forced interstitial ads from the game.

Children

Castle Fling is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data through a third-party service used by this app, please contact us and we will assist in addressing it.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA and similar laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal data, and the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. Because we operate no server and hold no personal data ourselves, most such requests concern data held by Google (Billing and AdMob), which you can manage through your Google account and the device settings above. You may also contact us at EmptyHelmetGames@gmail.com with any privacy request or question, and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles data (for example, by adding new services), we will update this policy and revise the effective date above.
